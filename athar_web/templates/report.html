Interpretation

  • A score at or above the model's threshold is reported as resistant. Thresholds were set on the DRIAMS-A training spectra (2015–2017). Intermediate results were counted as resistant.
  • Largest effect is the region whose replacement by its training average changed the score most. A positive value means the region pushed the score towards resistant.
  • These results come from a research prototype. They must not guide treatment and must be confirmed by phenotypic antimicrobial susceptibility testing.
