ABOUT THE PROJECT

About ATHAR أثر

A research prototype for antimicrobial resistance prediction in Escherichia coli using MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry and machine learning.

Understanding ATHAR

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is a major challenge in modern healthcare. ATHAR explores whether mass spectrometry data can support computational prediction of antibiotic susceptibility in E. coli.

The project investigates spectral representations, feature-selection techniques, and machine-learning classifiers to identify informative regions associated with resistance patterns.

Dataset & Evaluation

Research design and validation protocol.

Dataset

ATHAR uses E. coli spectra from the DRIAMS-A dataset. Each spectrum is represented by 6,000 intensity bins spanning approximately 2,000–20,000 Da.

Ten consecutive bins form one spectral region, resulting in 600 candidate regions.

Evaluation Protocol

Samples from 2015–2017 form the development dataset, with five stratified cross-validation folds.

Data from 2018 is reserved as a temporal test set and excluded from model tuning.
