About ATHAR أثر

ATHAR is a research prototype that predicts antibiotic resistance in Escherichia coli from MALDI-TOF mass spectra. Laboratories already record these spectra to identify the species, so a resistance signal from the same spectrum could arrive a day or two before phenotypic susceptibility testing is complete.

ATHAR is not a clinically validated diagnostic device. Its output must not guide treatment or replace antimicrobial susceptibility testing.

SpeciesEscherichia coli
Spectra7,320
Representation6,000 bins of 3 Da covering 2,000–20,000 Da, as preprocessed by DRIAMS
Regions600 regions of 10 consecutive bins (30 Da each)
LabelsSusceptible = 0; intermediate or resistant = 1
Training data2015–2017
Test data2018, held out from all model development

How a prediction is made

  1. The uploaded spectrum is checked: 6,000 finite, non-negative intensities.
  2. For each antibiotic with an installed model, the spectrum is standardized with the training-year statistics and reduced to the features that the model uses.
  3. The model returns a score, which is compared with the threshold set on the training years. A score at or above the threshold is reported as resistant.
  4. The calls are saved in the history with the sample ID and file name. The spectrum itself is not stored.
